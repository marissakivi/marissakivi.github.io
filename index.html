Hi I'm Marissa, and I bring clarity to dirty data.

Ever since I was a young child, I have loved solving puzzles and exploring the natural world around me. Today, I get to do both for a living.

I am a data scientist specializing in geospatial data, remote sensing, ecosystem modeling (process-based, ML) and data QA/QC pipelines, with 5+ years of experience working in agtech and carbon markets.

Good models require good data. In a fast-moving world where "vibe coding" is becoming the norm, I stand out by advocating for end-to-end data integrity and scientific rigor. My initiative to trace data origins, audit pipelines, and resolve hidden flaws has consistently led me to internal data governance consulting roles. In these positions, I have investigated critical end-to-end data-model pipelines, documented key risks, and delivered recommendations to internal teams that drove real operational change -- all on top of the rest of my day-to-day responsibilities.

If you are looking for someone to bring clarity to an opaque or messy data pipeline, let's connect! Check out my resume below.

Outside of work, I enjoy bouldering and sport climbing, dog training, running, and reading. I currently live and work in the San Francisco Bay Area with my partner and our dog, Zoey.
